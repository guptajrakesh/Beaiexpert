About BeAIExpert

Free, in-depth AI education for everyone — written to be understood, not just skimmed.

Our mission

AI is changing how people work, learn and build — but most explanations are either too shallow to be useful or too technical to follow. BeAIExpert exists to close that gap. We explain how AI really works, the technology stack behind it, and how to build with it, in clear language and real depth.

Everything on this site is free to read, with no account, paywall or sign-up.

Who we are

BeAIExpert is written and maintained by the BeAIExpert Team, a group focused on practical AI education. We write for curious beginners, working professionals and developers alike — anyone who wants to understand AI well enough to use it wisely and build with it confidently.

What we cover

  • AI Fundamentals — how large language models, embeddings, attention and multimodal AI actually work.
  • The AI Tech Stack — every layer of a modern AI system, from compute and models to vector databases and serving.
  • AI Development — hands-on guides with working code: RAG applications, agents, evaluation and more.
  • Responsible AI — the real risks and the practical habits that keep people and organizations safe.

Start with all articles, or look up a term in the AI Glossary.

Our principles

  • Free for everyone. Knowledge about AI shouldn't sit behind a paywall.
  • Depth over hype. We explain mechanisms and trade-offs, not buzzwords.
  • Practical. Development articles include working code examples, tested wherever possible before publishing.
  • Vendor-neutral. We explain concepts that work across providers and name tools only to illustrate options. We don't accept payment for coverage.
  • Accurate and current. Articles show when they were last updated, and we revise them as the field evolves.

Corrections and feedback

AI moves fast and we're not infallible. If you spot an error, an outdated detail, or something that could be explained better, please email hello@beaiexpert.com. We review every report and update articles when we get something wrong.
